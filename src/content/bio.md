I am a postdoc in the [Quantum Field Theory group](https://www.mpp.mpg.de/en/research/structure-of-matter/quantum-field-theory) of [Johannes Henn](https://www.mpp.mpg.de/en/about-us/organization/directors/prof-dr-johannes-henn) at the [Max Planck Institute for Physics](https://www.mpp.mpg.de/en/) in Garching.

I received my PhD from the [University of Hamburg](https://www.uni-hamburg.de/en.html), where I worked with [Sven-Olaf Moch](https://www.physik.uni-hamburg.de/en/th2/ag-moch/about-group-head.html) on [the use of critical conformal invariance in the renormalization of twist-2 operators](https://ediss.sub.uni-hamburg.de/handle/ediss/12508). My PhD position was funded by the [ERC Advanced Grant "Conformal-EIC"](https://www.fis.uni-hamburg.de/en/preise/detail.html?id=9768c059-87b1-488d-9c30-bf44feb395ba).

My undergraduate research was centered around the St. Petersburg Department of the [Steklov Mathematical Institute](https://www.pdmi.ras.ru/pdmi/en) under the supervision of [Sergey Derkachov](https://www.pdmi.ras.ru/pdmi/en/staff/sergei-derkachev), where I worked on applying [conformal symmetry](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0370269322002842) and [integrability](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10958-022-05998-3) to the evaluation of multiloop Feynman diagrams.
